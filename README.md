# FifTeam Media

A music app for Android and iPhone that runs from the web and installs to the home screen like a normal app:

- **Player**: your own music, copied into the app so it is there every time (works offline). The cover art tilts the way you tilt the phone, with a blurred glow and motion blur. Lock-screen controls, shuffle, repeat, up next. BASS, MIDS and HIGHS knobs: each one changes its own band and nothing else (a band turned up that would clip is held back on its own, never the others). The lock beside them stops accidental touches.
- **Rebass**: takes the song's own bass out completely and puts clean, loud sine notes in its place, inside the range you pick (two handles: whichever is higher is the top; put both on the same frequency and every bass note plays on that one frequency), at the level you pick (0 dB to −7.5 dB, −5 dB to start with). The bass is separated from everything else by Demucs v4 (an AI source separator) running on the phone itself, on its graphics chip (WebGPU) when it has one; its notes are found on that clean bass alone, so a voice or a guitar can't become a bass note, and the vocals and drums are left as they were. The song and its vocals keep exactly their own level; the chosen range belongs to the new bass (the song's own content below its top, up to 90 Hz, comes out), so those notes are the main thing down there. Every new note is at the bass level you choose (−5 dB means every note at −5 dB, however loud or quiet the original note was), full while the original note sounds, with the original's own timing, gaps and re-hits. Zero clipping is guaranteed: a look-ahead gain that is proven to stay under the ceiling, then a true-peak (between-samples) check. A live graph shows the bass being added. **Custom notes** (the tab next to Auto bass): the song's bass is taken out the same way, then you place the new bass by hand on a note grid over the song (time across, each row's frequency in Hz up the side, the song's own bass line faintly behind): tap to place a note, drag to move it, drag its edge to make it longer, pinch to zoom, or start from the song's own notes; it plays as you edit, and each song's notes are kept on the phone. Drag along the whole-song strip to move through it. The Intro switch puts the FifTeam Production tag (`media/intro.wav`) at the start, or (Premium) your own: record your voice (up to 15 seconds), slow it (50 to 100%, same pitch) and pitch it (up or down 12 semitones, same speed), hear it, and it goes before every song you rebass with the intro on (kept on the phone). Name it, then save it to the phone, share it (a file too big for the share sheet goes as an MP3), or keep it in the library. WAV 16/24-bit, FLAC (lossless, about half the size of a WAV) or MP3 320.
- **Social**: pictures, videos and rebassed songs from everyone using the app, with likes and comments (open them with a post's comments button; reply to a comment, like one). Tap someone's picture to send them a private message; all your conversations are behind the messages button next to Settings. Everything is live: a new message, like, comment or post shows up on everyone's phone the moment it happens, and Messages opens at once. Real notifications (even with the app closed): new messages, and likes, comments, replies, follows and requests, each switched on or off in Settings > Notifications (on iPhone, once the app is on the Home Screen). The bell shows your activity: who liked or commented on your posts, and who replied to or liked your comments; tap one to go to it. Messages are encrypted on the phones, so only the two people talking can read them. Group chats (New group, in Messages): up to 50 people, sealed with the group's own key so only its members can read it; admins rename it, add and remove people (a new key for the ones left), make admins and delete any message; members leave; the owner deletes it; the server checks every change against the account that made it. Send photos and videos in a chat too (sealed the same way). Tap the name at the top of a chat for their page; its ⋯ sees their page, reports or blocks them. Hold a message to delete it for both of you, or hold a conversation in Messages to delete the whole chat. **Send** (on every post) sends the post to someone in a message; a rebassed song can be sent from the Rebass screen or from the library (sealed the same way: only the two of you can play it, and they can add it to their library). A post can have up to 10 pictures and videos: they show as a collage, a tap opens the post's own feed of all of them, and a tap on one goes full screen, where they swipe from one to the next. A picture can have music: a 15- or 30-second part of any song in your library, heard before posting, played from the ♪ under the poster's name. **Rebasses** (a tab next to Feed): Community Rebass, only the rebassed songs people have posted (and their rebass playlists), apart from the feed, sorted by newest, top this week or most liked; a song that ends plays the next one down. Rebassed songs can be voted for (🔥 Vote); the one with the most votes from the last seven days sits on top as **Rebass of the week**. Tap anyone's picture or name for their page: followers and who they follow, their about me and their setup (subs, amps, vehicle, and their loudest in dB), Follow and Message, and their posts in two tabs (pictures and videos, rebassed songs). Your own page (your picture in the top bar, on every screen) changes your picture, username, about me and setup; a new username shows on your older posts too. Their page also shows how many likes their posts have had from others, and has Block (you won't see each other, and they can't message or follow you; unblock there or in Settings > Privacy). **Report** (on someone's page, or in a post's ⋯ menu): pick what for (spam, harassment, hate speech, nudity, violence, a scam, pretending to be someone, under 13, something else) and add your own message; it goes to the FifTeam team, who never say who sent it. The FifTeam tags (Founder, Media Developer, FifTeam Advertiser, Member) show next to the name everywhere: the feed, comments and their page; FifTeam Premium shows by itself on anyone with Premium. A new post or comment slides in without the feed being drawn again: what's on screen stays put (pictures don't reload, a song or video playing keeps going), and scrolled down the feed, a New posts button takes you up to them. **Audio Market** (a tab in Social): buy and sell audio gear. A listing has up to 4 photos, a title, a price, a category, its condition, a description, and where it is: the phone's location (it asks), shared only as the town and a spot rounded to about 3 miles, so buyers see how far away it is; filter by distance, price and category, sort by newest, closest or price. Message seller opens a chat about that listing, kept under Audio Market in Messages. Settings > Privacy: who can message you (everyone, by request, or nobody), and a private account (only followers you let in see your posts; new followers ask first, under the bell). Pick a username and a profile picture, post. A post can be Public or Only me (private: only you see it); hold one of your posts down on your page (or use its ⋯) to switch. Your page has a Private tab and a Liked tab (the posts you've liked; others see it only if you switch on Settings > Privacy > Show the posts I've liked). When someone sends your post to a friend, you're told. Pull the feed down to refresh it. Posts are kept on the FifTeam feed (`social/config.json`, set up with `tools/social_setup.mjs`).
- **Library categories**: in Rebassed, make categories with names and cover pictures of your choosing, and put each rebass in as many as you like (a rebass's menu > Add to a category). Share a category to Social as a playlist (its menu > Share to Social): anyone can play its songs or add them all. Playlists can't be voted for.
- **The way in**: the app only runs installed on a phone (opened from the home screen). In a browser tab, or on a computer, it shows nothing but how to install it (iPhone: Safari's Share, then Add to Home Screen; Android: Install app). Installed, it asks for an account (sign in, or create one), then a username, under "What should we call you?" (an account that has one on another phone takes it; a name someone else has is refused). Nothing can be used until all three are done, and only then does the phone check in as online.
- **Accounts**: the app opens on a sign-in screen until it's signed in with an email and a password (once: it stays signed in; Sign out is at the top of Settings). A new account confirms its email with a link sent to it; there's a forgotten-password link too. Accounts are Google's free Firebase Authentication (social/config.json "firebase"), so nothing has to be running for anyone to sign up or sign in. A phone signed in to an account has the account's profile, posts and likes; an account banned by email is shut on all its phones.
- **Going back**: a swipe in from the left edge (or the phone's own back) closes the full-screen viewer, then an open panel, then goes to the screen before.
- **Premium**: $10, once, through PayPal or Cash App ($ThirdEyeDragon999), for the account on every phone it signs in on: unlimited rebassing (free: 10 different songs an hour; the same song again, with other settings, doesn't count again), unlimited downloads (free: 5 songs an hour, added from Social or a message, or saved as a file), 4th and 6th-order boxes in Box, and a custom intro (your own voice before your songs). It shows up where it matters: tap a locked feature (4th order, say) or reach a limit and the Premium sheet says what it unlocks; Settings > Premium too. It unlocks once the payment's confirmed.
- **Rules and legal**: Settings > Rules and legal has the Terms of Use and Rules (FifTeam Media doesn't provide music; what you make is for your own listening and can't be distributed or sold; no posting other DJs' rebasses; changing or tampering with the app is grounds for a ban).
- **Kept civil**: racial slurs (in any spelling) can't be posted, commented, messaged or listed; swearing is fine.
- **Box**: a sub box designer. Sealed, ported, 4th-order and 6th-order bandpass, in standard, series (6th) or hallway style (the subs facing each other on two dividers), for 1 to 20 subs. Pick from 310 real subs with their published Thiele/Small numbers (Skar, Sundown, DD Audio, B2, Resilient Sounds, Rockford Fosgate, Kicker, American Bass, Alpine, CT Sounds, DS18, Deaf Bonce, Crescendo, Hertz, Pioneer, Orion; the numbers a maker doesn't publish are filled with typical values and named; square subs like Kicker's L7s drawn square, with a square cutout; Resilient's Platinum and Gold V2s, whose Thiele/Small numbers aren't published, are flagged as typical), typical 8–18″ subs, or your own sub's numbers (Vas and Sd in litres and cm² or ft³ and in²; a number that can't be right, like a spec in the wrong unit, is caught with a one-tap fix). Quick builds design the whole box in one tap (Daily, Deep, Loud, Windy, SPL, Compact, SQ, Bandpass, Hallway), each searched for the sub picked at your amp's power: box sizes and tunings scored on what the sub can really play there, so it uses all of its excursion and never goes past its Xmax (above the subsonic filter) when any box can hold it; Windy moves the most air through a huge port, SPL makes the highest peak, Loud the most from 35 to 70 Hz, Deep the lowest notes it plays cleanly; or recommended volumes and tunings, or your own. The box sizes itself to the volume and shapes itself so the subs and ports fit (inside the space you have, if you give it), or keeps your exact measurements; wedges too; a ported box's port opens where you choose: out of the front, the back, the top or a side, on the left, the right or in the middle (between the subs, half each side), its opening cut in that panel, long ones folded back on themselves clear of the subs; slot ports bent once or twice when they're long (with a 45° strip in each bend so the air never turns a square 90°), or round ports; the subs spaced as far apart as you like and never in a port's way; dowel bracing worked out from the wood and the power, placed clear of the subs and ports; the baffle the subs sit on (the front, or a bandpass box's divider) in as many layers of wood as you want. A live 3D view to turn and zoom (solid, see-through, open top; MDF, carpet or any paint colour; every box carries a FifTeam Media engraving on its side), the simulated response (checked against the textbook transfer functions), cone travel, port air speed, impedance and group delay at your amp's power, what to watch out for, the cut list, wiring for any number of subs, saved designs, and sharing a box on Social, where anyone can open it. My system (the second part of Box): what you're running (head unit or factory radio, subs and their coils, mono and 4-channel amps, speakers in every door, a lithium bank, alternator, Big 3, a bass knob, voltmeters), worked out (the draw at full power, from each amp's rating rather than how its speakers happen to be wired, so every fuse is at least what the amp can pull and never more than its wire takes; the power run and every fuse, how long the bank lasts, the subs wired for the amps' lowest stable load, the speakers on the amps' channels) and drawn as a wiring diagram of the car: tap a part to light up its wires (a bus bar lists what goes on each post), tap a wire to see where it starts and ends, its gauge and fuse; the sub box shows each amp's subs wired coil by coil. A rear battery bank of lithium or AGM batteries (any number), bus bars only if you have them (otherwise a fused distribution block, each amp grounded right by it), and every heavy wire and fuse with its thinner equivalent (3/0, or 2 runs of 1/0 on 2 × 200 A fuses). The cut list has Show preview: the box put together in the 3D view piece by piece (bottom, back, sides, divider, port, bracing, front, top, subs), each step with its measurements; pick the step you're on, or play them all.
- **Tones**: test tones from 1 Hz to 20 kHz, square/triangle/saw, white and pink noise, level in dBFS, left/right/both, polarity flip, log or linear sweeps; save tones as files too.
- On Android you can also share a song from any app (Files, a downloader, a chat) straight to FifTeam Media.

## Put it on your phone

- **Android (Chrome)**: open the link above and tap **Install** (top of the Library screen, or the browser menu > Install app).
- **iPhone (Safari)**: open the link, tap **Share > Add to Home Screen**. Open it from the home screen; the first time on the Player, tap **Turn on tilt** to allow motion.

Saving on iPhone goes through the share sheet: choose **Save to Files**. Only rebassed songs are shared from the app (a file, a locked file, or a rebass code).

## The FifTeam bot

`tools/bot/fifteam-bot.mjs` gives the FifTeam server's admins four commands: `/online` (who has the app open now, with their device and app version), `/ban <user>` (shuts the app on that phone until `/unban`; a username bans every phone using it), `/unban <user>` and `/bans`, plus moderating: `/posts` and `/comments` (the latest, or one user's), `/delete-post`, `/delete-comment`, `/purge <user>` (all their posts and comments) and `/clear-profile <user>` (their picture and about me; `/allow-picture` lets a picture back); `/devices` (every phone that has used the app in the last half year, with its device, the full list as a file); and the gradient tags next to a name: `/tag-member`, `/tag-founder`, `/tag-developer`, `/tag-advertiser` (with `remove` to take one off) and `/tags`. Accounts: `/accounts` (every account, online or not, with its phone, app version and when it was last on; the app keeps the list, each email sealed so only the bot can read it), `/ban-email` and `/unban-email` (bans are kept as email fingerprints, and work with the PC off). Reports from the app arrive in the server's #reports channel (who, by whom, why, the post or comment, and their message; at most 8 an hour from one account). For Audio Market: `/listings` and `/delete-listing` (and `/purge` takes a user's listings down too). Unlimited rebassing: `/premiums` (who has it, and who has tapped Pay; the owner is also told in the server when someone does) and `/premium <user>` (unlock it once the $10 is in PayPal or Cash App; `remove` takes it away). Every half hour it also keeps every post's file links fresh in one place, so phones load Social with one request instead of one per post. It runs online on Cloudflare Workers (free; `tools/bot/worker.mjs`), together with the FifTeam server (`tools/bot/hub.mjs`): the app's data in one place, a phone getting what it needs in one quick request, changes that can't overwrite each other, live updates and push notifications. So nothing has to be running on a PC: set it up once with `node tools/bot/fifteam-bot.mjs --setup`, then `--online` (the steps are at the top of that file). A ban is tied to the phone's app data: someone who clears FifTeam Media's data (or reinstalls) comes back as a new phone.

## Hosting your own copy

This copy is already live at the link above. To host another one: the app has to be on an `https://` address for phones to install it (and to allow the tilt sensor and sharing). Any free static host works. Upload **everything in this folder except `test/` and `tools/`** (including `models/` and `vendor/`: the bass separator).

**GitHub Pages (free, about 5 minutes)**
1. Make a free account at github.com, then a new public repository, for example `fifteam-media`.
2. On the repository page choose **Add file > Upload files** and drag in the contents of this folder (index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, and the css, js and icons folders). Commit.
3. **Settings > Pages**: Source **Deploy from a branch**, branch **main**, folder **/ (root)**. Save.
4. After a minute it is live at `https://<your-name>.github.io/fifteam-media/`.

**Netlify**: sign in at app.netlify.com, then drag this folder onto the "deploy manually" drop area.

## Tests

- `node test/dsp.test.mjs`: the rebass engine on a made-up song mastered to the ceiling: pitch accuracy, range, and zero clipping at 0, −3 and −7.5 dB.
- `node test/bass.test.mjs`: the bass-note finder, the mapping, the new bass and the mastering, on made-up songs with a known bass line.
- `node test/eq.test.mjs`: the player's knobs measured in headless Edge: each knob moves only its own band, and a band turned up on a loud song is held back alone, never over full scale.
- `node test/social.test.mjs`: FifTeam Social against a stand-in feed service (username, feed, posting a picture and a rebass, playing and keeping someone's song, deleting, a post deleted on the service, markup in a post, no pings) and the knob lock.
- `node test/hub.test.mjs`: the FifTeam server in Cloudflare's own runtime on this computer (wrangler dev) against stand-ins: stores in one request, changes only on the latest copy, the copy back, older apps' writes merged in, the live connection, push notifications (decrypted to check them), files for many posts at once, the unlock request and `/premium`.
- `node test/system.test.mjs`: My system's numbers: every amp fused for its rating (a 5000 W amp for its 450-odd amps, whatever it's wired to), every fuse at least its draw and never more than its wire takes, in nearly 3,000 builds.
- `node test/groups.test.mjs`: group chats' rules on the server: who may make, rename, add to, remove from, leave and delete a group, make admins, and send or delete its messages; an older app's write can't wipe them.
- `node test/live.test.mjs`: two phones using the app through that server: how fast a message arrives and Messages opens, no per-post requests, a real push shown by the phone, a comment's notification, and unlimited rebassing from the limit to the unlock.
- `node test/sep.test.mjs`: the bass separator's own maths (spectrogram, segments, resampling), the clean-up of the old bass's traces, and the note finder on a bass alone.
- `node test/make-songs.mjs <dir>` then `node test/serve.mjs` and `node test/e2e.mjs http://localhost:8123/ <dir> <out>`: drives the whole app in headless Edge at phone size (import, play, tilt, rebass, save WAV and MP3, tones) and checks the saved files.

## Updating

After changing any file, raise `VERSION` in `sw.js` (for example `fifteam-v2`) so installed copies pick up the change.
